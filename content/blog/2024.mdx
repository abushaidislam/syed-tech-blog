## 1. Dub Analytics 2.0

This year, we gave our Analytics product a [major facelift](/blog/introducing-analytics-2.0) by introducing the following improvements:

- [New Filter UX](/blog/introducing-analytics-2.0#new-filter-ux)
- ["Ask AI" feature](/help/article/dub-analytics#bonus-ask-ai-feature)
- [Date Range Picker](/blog/introducing-analytics-2.0#date-range-picker)

Throughout this year, we've also been constantly improving our Analytics product:

- [Creating public analytics dashboards](/changelog/dashboard-sharing)
- [Filtering by continent](/changelog/continents-support)
- [Filtering by regions](/changelog/regions-support)
- [Filtering by QR code scan vs link click](/help/article/filter-analytics-by-trigger)

## 2. Real-time Events Stream

We also launched [Real-time Events Stream](/help/article/real-time-events-stream) – a detailed, real-time stream of events across your entire Dub workspace.

The real-time events stream lives in your [workspace's Events tab](https://app.dub.co/events) and is available to all Business plan users on Dub.

## 3. Free .LINK Domains

In September, we also partnered with [Nova Registry](https://nova.link/) to offer a **1-year free `.link` domain** to all paying Dub customers.

This domain is a great way for you to get started with [custom domains](/blog/custom-domains) and create short links that match your brand. `.link` domains are also more trustworthy and can lead to **30% higher click-through rates** than generic short links.

## 4. Dub Integrations

This year, we also launched [Dub Integrations](/blog/introducing-dub-integrations), a new way to extend Dub's functionality with apps like [Zapier](https://app.dub.co/integrations/zapier), [Raycast](https://app.dub.co/integrations/raycast), [Make.com](https://app.dub.co/integrations/make), and more.

Dub also supports [OAuth 2.0 authentication](https://dub.co/docs/integrations/quickstart) for building your own integrations. To help you get started, we've built a simple [example integration](https://oauth.dub.sh) that you can use as a reference.

## 5. Dub API General Availability

This year, we also [launched our Dub API](/blog/announcing-dub-api) to general availability. Since the beginning of 2024, companies like Twilio, Perplexity, Whop, and Raycast have been using our [API](/docs/api-reference/introduction) to create short links programmatically.

## 6. New Link Builder

 In October, we launched a new version of our [link builder](/help/article/dub-link-builder) - rebuilt from the ground up – to help you manage your links better.

Some of the improvements include:

- [Enhanced domain and tag selectors](/blog/new-link-builder#enhanced-domain-and-tag-selectors)
- [QR codes as a first-class citizen](/blog/new-link-builder#qr-codes-as-a-first-class-citizen)
- [Custom link previews (with AI)](/blog/new-link-builder#custom-link-previews-with-ai)
- [Persisted link drafts](/blog/new-link-builder#persisted-link-drafts)
- [Keyboard shortcuts](/blog/new-link-builder#keyboard-shortcuts)

## 7. New Dashboard Design

This year, we also gave our dashboard a [major facelift](/blog/new-links-dashboard).

Not only did we redesign our links dashboard, we also moved to a [sidebar navigation
layout](/changelog/new-sidebar-nav) to make our dashboard more powerful and support
our growing product offering in the future.

## 8. SOC 2 Type II Compliance

We're also excited to share that Dub is now [SOC 2 Type II compliant](/blog/soc2).

This is a major milestone for us as it demonstrates our commitment to maintaining the highest standards of security, privacy, and reliability for our customers.

## 9. UTM Templates

We also launched [UTM Templates](/changelog/utm-templates) to help you streamline UTM campaign management across your team.

UTM templates can be easily accessed via the [link builder](/help/article/dub-link-builder) by your entire team, which makes it easier to create and manage UTM campaigns. Learn more about [how to create UTM templates](/help/article/how-to-create-utm-templates).

## 10. CSV Import

Last but not least, we also launched a [CSV Import](/help/article/how-to-import-csv) feature to help you easily import links from a CSV file.

This is useful for customers who are migrating from another link management platform that we don't have [native migration tooling](/help/category/migrating) for.

## Massive growth on all fronts

Thanks to your support, we had a record year in 2024 on all our key metrics:

- **126M clicks tracked** (+900%)
- **700K links created** (+400%)
- **56K new users** (+360%)
- **5.5K custom domains** (+500%)

We also grew to a global team of 4 and are excited to see what we can build together
in 2025.
