Today, we achieved a major milestone: $10,000,000 in payouts sent via [Dub Partners](/partners) to creators worldwide – in just 6 months.

It's a testament to the trust that our customers have in us to track, reward, and pay out their partners – as well as the caliber of partners that are in our partner network.

## 1-click global payouts + tax compliance

From day one, our goal with [Dub Partners](/partners) has been to make [partner payouts](/help/article/partner-payouts) effortless: You pay one single invoice, and Dub automatically routes your funds to *tens of thousands* of partners globally.

[Performing payouts in Dub](/help/article/partner-payouts) has streamlined our payout process and is a breeze. We get [real insights into our top-performing partners](/help/article/program-analytics), filtered by month, quarter, etc.

## New partner engagement features

Over the last few months, we've also added countless features to help you increase partner engagement:

1. [Email campaigns](/help/article/email-campaigns): Send marketing and transactional emails to your partners.
2. [Messaging center](/help/article/messaging-partners): Communicate with your partners directly within Dub.
3. [Program bounties](/help/article/program-bounties): Create performance and submission bounties to drive partner engagement.
4. [Application form builder](/help/article/program-application-form): Customize your application form to onboard high-quality partners to your program.
5. [Partner discount codes](/changelog/discount-codes): Offer dual-sided incentives and track partner conversions – no links required.

We are also testing out our Partner Network feature with select Enterprise customers – [feel free to reach out](/contact/sales) if you're interested in growing your program with Dub Enterprise.
