Dub offers a comprehensive toolkit for creating [branded short links](https://dub.co/features/branded-links), managing [UTM parameters](https://dub.co/help/article/utm-builder), and customizing [link previews](https://dub.co/help/article/custom-link-previews) - providing value across all its [plans](https://dub.co/pricing):

2. **Streamlined UTM Tracking:** Dub makes it easy to add UTM parameters to links for effective campaign tracking. With features like [reusable templates](https://dub.co/help/article/how-to-create-utm-templates) and automation, even free-tier users can save time and avoid manual errors.

### Bitly: Key Features Locked Behind Paywalls

While Bitly offers branded short links and basic customization, its most valuable features are restricted to higher-priced plans:

- **Branded Short Links:** The free plan only allows the use of Bitly’s default domain, which can harm your campaign’s credibility and click-through rates, especially since such domains are often associated with spam. Notably, [AT&T](https://asecare.att.com/tutorials/3-tips-on-sending-links-in-sms-%E2%94%82-att-officehand-95343/?product=AT&T%20Office@Hand#:~:text=2.%20Rethink%20link%20shortening%20services) actively blocks such generic domains like bit.ly. To access one custom domain, users must upgrade to the $35/mo Growth plan, while multi-domain support is only available at the Enterprise level. The Growth plan allows users to create up to 500 links/mo.
- **UTM Parameters:** Free users cannot manually add UTM parameters, a critical feature for tracking campaign performance. This functionality becomes available on the $8/mo Core plan, while preset UTM templates require the $35/mo Growth plan.It's important to note that the $8/mo plan is only available when billed annually.
- **Social Media Previews:** Bitly lacks built-in options for customizing link previews, such as titles, descriptions, or images. Users must modify the destination page’s metadata manually to control how links appear on social platforms.

### Rebrandly: Generous features at an affordable price

Rebrandly offers a compelling mix of features at an attractive price point, standing out as a more accessible option compared to Bitly. However, it does have some limitations:

1. **Branded Short Links:** The free plan includes branded short links but supports **only one custom domain**. To manage multiple domains, users must upgrade to the $39/mo Professional plan, which allows connecting up to three domains and provides 1500 links/mo.
2. **UTM Parameter Builder:** A notable advantage of Rebrandly is its inclusion of a UTM parameter builder in the free plan, enabling users to track campaign performance without additional costs.
3. **Social Media Previews:** Rebrandly does not support customizable social media previews. Users cannot edit link metadata, such as titles, images, or descriptions, regardless of the plan.

### Verdict

- Dub and Rebrandly are the clear winners in link creation and management, with Dub having a better edge. Dub offers the most links/month, multiple custom domains, and customizable link previews, even on its free plan, making it the most flexible choice.
- Bitly falls behind, with essential features locked behind expensive plans, making it less appealing overall.
- Rebrandly is a great alternative, but it limits users to one custom domain and lacks social media preview options, giving Dub the advantage.
